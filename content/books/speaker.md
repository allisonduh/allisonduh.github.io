**Title:** Speaker for the Dead <br>
**Author:** Orson Scott Card<br>

My favorite sequel I have ever read. It is tonally, atmospherically, literally set worlds away from [[endersgame|Ender's Game]] and grapples with xenophobia and atonement. I don't recommend the two remaining books in the series, although it's been more than a few years since I tried.

If you like this, consider picking up a copy of [Dawn](https://search.worldcat.org/title/1263278259), the first book of Octavia Butler's Lilith's Brood Trilogy, although I warn you it will be a wild ride.
